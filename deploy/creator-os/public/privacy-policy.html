Privacy Policy

Effective: September 23, 2026

This policy describes how AutomationOpsAI, operated by Luis Alberto Sánchez Arenas, processes information when creators connect YouTube or TikTok and use the video-production and publishing service.

Information we process

  • Account identifiers and basic profile information returned by an authorized platform, such as TikTok open ID, creator username or YouTube channel identifier.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens. Tokens are encrypted at rest and are not displayed in the browser after authorization.
  • Video files, thumbnails, captions, titles, tags, publishing preferences and processing status supplied to or produced by the service.
  • Operational records needed to diagnose uploads, retries, moderation results and failures.
  • The administrative token entered in the dashboard is stored in the user's browser local storage and sent only to the AutomationOpsAI backend for authentication.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate the creator, produce and validate requested media, publish content according to the creator's instructions, display status and analytics, prevent duplicate publication, secure the service and diagnose errors.

Platform access

YouTube and TikTok data is accessed only after OAuth authorization and only through the permissions shown by those platforms. AutomationOpsAI does not request or store the creator's platform password. Authorization can be revoked from the applicable Google or TikTok account settings.

Disclosure and service providers

Information is transmitted to Google/YouTube or TikTok only when required for an authorized operation. Hosting, database and network providers may process encrypted credentials, media and operational data to run the service. Personal information is not sold.

Retention and deletion

Publishing records and media are retained while needed to operate the workflow, prevent duplication and resolve failures. A creator may request deletion of stored account tokens, media and publishing records through the contact method below. Platform copies already published must be removed using the corresponding platform or its API.

Security

The service uses encrypted OAuth-token storage, signed media URLs, access controls and integrity checks. No internet service can guarantee absolute security; credentials should be revoked immediately if unauthorized access is suspected.

Children

The service is intended for adults and professional creators and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new effective date.

Contact and requests

For privacy questions, access or deletion requests, open a private-safe request through the project owner's GitHub profile. Do not include passwords, access tokens or other secrets.
